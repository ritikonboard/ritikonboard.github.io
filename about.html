About

Namaste Ji, I'm Ritik Vaishnav.

I am currently an undergraduate student exploring research opportunities in autonomous vehicle perception. Previously, I worked as an undergraduate researcher under Dr. Arani Bhattacharya at the WISE Lab, IIIT Delhi, where I focused on enhancing perception capabilities and remote supervision streaming systems for autonomous driving.

Beyond academics and engineering, I am passionate about filmmaking, camera systems, traveling, and Hindi literature. I value a simple, slow-paced life—chasing sunrises, exploring mountains, and sharing moments over a cup of chai.
